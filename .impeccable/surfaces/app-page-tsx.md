# Home and public marketing surfaces

Scope: the public site of BLF Lab's (home, services, offer pages, pricing, method, references, about, FAQ, contact, order wizard chrome, footer). Visitor mode: Persuade. /admin is out of scope and follows the shared tokens only.

Audience and job: small business owners and practitioners in France, often on a phone, who decide within seconds whether to describe their project. Action: start the wizard on /commander. Proof: two live projects with screenshots, a written four-stage method, a published pricing logic. No reviews, logos or metrics exist, and none may be invented.

Carte blanche: Adam gave carte blanche for the redesign on 2026-09-15, so no interview round was held and the assigned direction was built unattended.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A project with BLF Lab's is a line you ride from framing to a terminus where the keys are handed over in your name. The page is a line diagram, not an agency hero with a 3D object and a neon accent.

OWN-WORLD: A wayfinding line-map system on enamel white panels. Thick flat line strokes with round caps, white station roundels with a dark ring, and station names set as condensed placard caps. Four named line colours, one per offer family (vermilion, violet from the logo apostrophe, green, blue), with an ink-black plate for signs. No gradients, no glass, no shadows. States resolve lit or unlit, with no midtone.

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, sees the whole ride on one line (framing, mockup, development, hand-over of the keys), recognises the terminus as the offer itself, then boards through "Démarrer un projet".

FIRST VIEWPORT: On desktop, a left column (7 of 12) carries the H1 verbatim at display scale, the paragraph, and two actions: a violet plate "Démarrer un projet" and a ruled "Voir les services". A right column (5 of 12) carries "Ligne BLF", a vertical line of four stations top to bottom, the terminus as an oversized roundel listing code, nom de domaine and accès. On mobile the line follows the actions, still vertical.

FORM: Transit line diagram and wayfinding signage, candidate 4 of 7 (1 site-board, 2 enamel street plaque, 3 service booklet, 4 line diagram, 5 key tags, 6 architect plans, 7 key-cutting board). Seed key 12610d13.

Raises:
- From the cabaret bill (declined): no midtone, a station is lit or unlit, and the active nav item reverses to a solid plate.
- From the industrial quote grammar (competitive, clarity): wayfinding is reading, and every zone carries its literal name on a placard.
- From the night flight six-pack (declined): a cross-check, where every claim sits beside its verifiable proof link.
- From the mezzotint plate (declined): total commitment of the ground, with one panel colour per theme and no third surface.

Signature interaction: the hero line draws from origin to terminus once on load, and the stations light in sequence. Content stays visible by default and reduced motion shows the finished line.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
